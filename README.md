## About Me

I am an Informatics student based in **Malang, Indonesia**, specializing as a **Data Scientist** and **Data Analyst**. I am passionate about uncovering hidden insights within complex datasets, building predictive models, and translating data into strategic solutions.

My philosophy: *"Data is using the past for a better future."* I combine structured statistics, programming, and analytics to solve data problems, while also integrating IoT and web technologies for telemetry pipelines.
